## The question, stated plainly

FinLens is currently optimizing for two goals that pull in different directions:

- **A · Portfolio artifact** — a demonstration of buy-side-grade analytical rigor
  for a VC career. The product IS the casebook + Integrity Engine.
- **B · Commercial SaaS** — a product real users pay for ($5–10k MRR target).
  The product is user-facing features on top of the data layer.

Both are legitimate. The cost is running them *simultaneously*: it lets moat work
feel like product progress while no user ever sees it. Pick one as PRIMARY; the
other becomes a byproduct, not a co-equal goal.

*(This is a framing tool. The choice is Riley's. The two columns are drawn
straight from the review's evidence so the trade-off is visible, not to steer.)*

## What the review said maps cleanly to each column

- *"What is genuinely scarce is the casebook, not the data"* → strengthens **A**.
- *"No fund manager buys a 15-name dataset"* + manual CAPTCHA ingestion ceiling →
  **B** is hard until ingestion + breadth are solved.
- *"The $199 anchor (Filing Diff) has sat Open and untouched for 5 weeks"* → if
  **B**, that's the next build, full stop.
- *"persona doc (fund manager) vs addendum ($49 student funnel) openly contradict"*
  → whichever you pick, one of those two documents gets formally shelved.
